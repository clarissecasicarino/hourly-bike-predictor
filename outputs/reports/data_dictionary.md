## Data Dictionary

- `season`: Season of the year (1:winter, 2:spring, 3:summer, 4:fall).
- `weekday`: Day of the week.
- `workingday`: 1 if the day is neither weekend nor holiday, otherwise 0.
- `weathersit`: Weather situation category.
  - 1: Clear, few clouds, partly cloudy.
  - 2: Mist and cloudy conditions.
  - 3: Light snow or light rain.
  - 4: Heavy rain, ice pallets, thunderstorm, or fog.

Source: [UCI Bike Sharing Dataset](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/275/bike+sharing+dataset)
